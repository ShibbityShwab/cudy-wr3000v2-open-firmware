net: mdio: luofu: add MDIO bus controller

Add the MDIO bus controller of the HiSilicon Hi5671Y "luofu" SoC: a
polled clause 22 master for the PHYs behind the internal switch
(addresses 2..5 on this board) with no interrupt.

The register model (0x20 done/acknowledge, 0x2c busy, 0x30 frame word,
0x38 read data) was recovered from the vendor hi_kmdio.ko module, and
this exact transaction sequence completed real clause 22 reads on the
SoC from an earlier out-of-tree 5.10 driver (the Genius PHY ID read back
as 0x20669a09).  This in-tree 6.18 version is a port of that sequence
and has been host-compiled only.

The gate clock and the seven reset lines come from the CRG provider; the
clock is enabled before the resets are deasserted.  That ordering is the
one assumption to confirm on the first hardware run, and it is stated in
the driver comment.

Host-compiled against linux-6.18.60, binding checked with
dt_binding_check; never run on hardware.

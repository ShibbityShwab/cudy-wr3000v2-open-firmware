net: ethernet: luofu-switch: add EQM queue-manager init (RFC)

The Hi5671Y switch's EQM (queue manager) block needs its queue/credit/shaping tables
programmed before the LSW datapath can enqueue. The vendor loader does this as part of
the attended W7 bring-up step; this patch makes the same table set part of the normal
boot init, so the switch comes up fully configured without a bench gate.

The tables and the init order are decoded from the stock firmware's W6/W7 sequences
(the EPS channel map and the EQM init); the register layout is the one the vendor's
own registers use on this silicon revision. [U] The exact queue/credit split was not
observed under concurrent traffic - the bring-up was exercised with the gate set and
mirror off, not with a live forwarding matrix.

The `luofu_lsw.qminit` bench option and the attended-gate comments are dropped: the
review form must carry no lab gates or module parameters (the acceptance rule).

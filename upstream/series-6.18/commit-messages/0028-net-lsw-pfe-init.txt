net: ethernet: luofu-switch: add PFE/IFC init and default tables (RFC)

The Hi5671Y switch's PFE (packet forwarding engine) needs its IFC (interface)
forwarding tables and the default-table set programmed before the exception
delivery path can classify frames. The vendor loader does this as three attended
W-bench stages; this patch merges them into the normal boot init.

The three source stages: the PFE init (the 036), the IFC default table (the 043)
and the IFC EPS/EXT fix-up (the 046). The table layout and the default entries are
decoded from the stock firmware's staged sequences. [U] The default-table content
was validated against the stock's readback but was not exercised with a full
forwarding matrix; the merge preserves the three stages' order.

The bench gates and the `luofu_lsw.*` options are dropped (the acceptance rule).

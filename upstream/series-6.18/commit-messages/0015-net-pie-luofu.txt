net: ethernet: hisilicon: add Luofu PIE packet engine draft

Add draft scaffolding for the PIE packet engine of the HiSilicon Hi5671Y
"luofu" SoC, the DMA/ring half of the wired data path.  The driver
claims the binding, validates the 64 KiB register window, the CRG
clock/reset and the four named interrupts, allocates the coherent RX/TX
descriptor rings and then refuses to arm the engine with -EOPNOTSUPP.

It touches no register, enables no clock, toggles no reset, requests no
interrupt and registers no NAPI instance.  The register/channel
inventory has never been captured on hardware, several ring-pair words
are only inferred, and an access to this block before it is ready has
hung the board bus.  The recovered offsets, descriptor accessors and IRQ
schedule are compiled scaffolding with the unrecovered fields marked
TODO.

This is an RFC and not mergeable: the attended read-only register
inventory is the next step and must precede any data-path code.  The
devicetree node stays disabled.  Never run on hardware.

Host-compiled against linux-6.18.60, binding checked with
dt_binding_check.

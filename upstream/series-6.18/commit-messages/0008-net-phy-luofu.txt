net: phy: add driver for HiSilicon Luofu "Genius" PHYs

Add a PHYLIB driver for the HiSilicon "Genius" clause 22 PHYs (PHY ID
0x20669a00, mask 0xffffff00) behind the internal switch of the Hi5671Y
"luofu" SoC.  Their clause 22 register set is standard, so the driver
uses the generic helpers and exists to bind the device to a named
SoC-owned driver instead of the generic PHY.

Not implemented, on purpose: the vendor per-PHY firmware patch
(hi_ephy_*.bin) is not loaded.  Its format and effect are not decoded,
and it is the first suspect if link training differs from the vendor
stack.  The PHY is matched by PHY ID, not by a devicetree compatible, so
the PHY child nodes stay standard ethernet-phy-ieee802.3-c22 and no
binding is added.

Host-compiled against linux-6.18.60; never run on hardware.

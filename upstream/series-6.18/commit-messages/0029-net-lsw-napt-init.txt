net: ethernet: luofu-switch: add NAPT flow-engine init (RFC)

The switch's NAPT (flow-engine) block needs its flow/aging tables initialised
before the engine can track flows. The vendor loader does this behind the attended
W-bench gate; this patch makes it a normal boot init, matching the state the M1a
proof image used (the decoded replay that produced native port deliveries).

[V] The init sequence was validated on hardware: the M1a proof image
(5800c9ab) replayed it and the switch delivered natively (ping 200/200, SSH).
[U] The flow-table sizing and the aging parameters beyond the proof run are
unmeasured.

The bench gate and the `luofu_switch.*` options are dropped (the acceptance rule).

net: ethernet: luofu-pie: add native exception-class RX delivery (RFC)

The PIE (packet interface engine) can deliver exception-class frames (the
control/protocol traffic the switch punts to the CPU) through its own RX path
instead of the accepterr bench option. This patch implements that native delivery
path and retires the accepterr option from the review form.

[V] The delivery was exercised with the exception path enabled on the bench line
(the E0-a mirror-off run and the M1a proof both delivered exception frames
natively). [U] The complete exception-class taxonomy and the per-class queue
selection are not fully measured.

The accepterr bench option is dropped (the acceptance rule).

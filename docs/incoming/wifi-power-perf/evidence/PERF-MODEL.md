# Derived performance model (wifi-power-perf, task-160)

Generated by tools/perf_model.py; every number is a **model**, not a
bench measurement. Grades: geometry [V], shipped values [V*], frame
timing [I]. The attended ladders in LADDER-BENCH.md are what promote a
row from model to [V*].

## 1. Host TX window (in-flight pool x completion poll)

The data-TX lane keeps at most HDTX_POOL_DEPTH frames in flight and
frees completions only when the delayed work runs (data_tx_poll_ms,
default 5): the host ceiling is pool x (1000 / poll_ms) frames/s of
MPDU bytes. MPDU <= 768 B, one TID, non-QoS, no aggregation.

| pool | poll_ms | frames/s | MPDU MB/s | payload Mbps (736 B app) |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 16 | 5 | 3200 | 2.46 | 18.8 |
| 16 | 2 | 8000 | 6.14 | 47.1 |
| 16 | 1 | 16000 | 12.29 | 94.2 |
| 32 | 5 | 6400 | 4.92 | 37.7 |
| 32 | 2 | 16000 | 12.29 | 94.2 |
| 32 | 1 | 32000 | 24.58 | 188.4 |
| 64 | 5 | 12800 | 9.83 | 75.4 |
| 64 | 2 | 32000 | 24.58 | 188.4 |
| 64 | 1 | 64000 | 49.15 | 376.8 |

Shipped default: pool 16 at 5 ms = **3200 frames/s = 2.46 MB/s MPDU =
18.8 Mbps of 736 B payload**.

## 2. Single-frame air ceiling (no aggregation)

HT-mixed 20 MHz 1SS frame timing model: preamble 40 us,
data = ceil(MPDU*8/PHY), SIFS+ACK+DIFS+avg-backoff = 156 us.

| MPDU B | PHY | frame us | cycle us | frames/s | payload Mbps |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 768 | 65 Mbps MCS7/20MHz/1SS | 135 | 291 | 3436 | 20.2 |
| 1500 | 65 Mbps MCS7/20MHz/1SS | 225 | 381 | 2624 | 30.8 |
| 768 | 130 Mbps MCS15/20MHz/2SS | 88 | 244 | 4098 | 24.1 |
| 1500 | 130 Mbps MCS15/20MHz/2SS | 133 | 289 | 3460 | 40.6 |
| 768 | 288 Mbps MCS9/HT40/2SS [I] | 62 | 218 | 4587 | 27.0 |
| 1500 | 288 Mbps MCS9/HT40/2SS [I] | 82 | 238 | 4201 | 49.3 |

Consequence: at 2.4 GHz MCS7 the non-aggregated air ceiling is ~3.4k
frames/s, i.e. the shipped host window (3.2k) is already within 6 % of
the air ceiling - the stack is **air-bound, not poll-bound**, at the
default 2.4 GHz rate. A faster PHY moves the bind to the host window:
raising the pool to the 64-entry ring depth makes the host window
12.8k frames/s at 5 ms. Aggregation (A-MPDU) is the only step that
removes the per-frame overhead, and it is not implemented in this
clean-room stack (firmware/BA session support unproven).

## 3. Scan pace (measured + batched model)

Measured: 156 ms/channel, firmware stops after
6 channels per g0 id0xC request. Driver batches
ceil(n/batch) requests with a 300 ms gap.

| channels | batch | requests | model wall time ms |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 6 | 6 | 1 | 936 |
| 13 | single | 1 | 2028 |
| 13 | 6 | 3 | 2628 |
| 13 | 7 | 2 | 2328 |
| 13 | 8 | 2 | 2328 |

A full 13-channel scan is structurally blocked at the DMAC (per the
sibling analysis): the 7/8-channel band-config rung (H1) is the
documented ceiling, so the batch=6 default plus gap is the correct
production shape; 13 channels are covered as 3 requests only if the
firmware re-arms its scan context (unproven).
